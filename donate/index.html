Become a Donator

Support PlanetNostalgia and help us keep the server online.

Support PlanetNostalgia

Donations help cover the cost of running the server. The Donator rank costs £8 and includes in-game perks as well as a Discord role.

Once you have donated, open a ticket in our Discord server and send us your Minecraft username.

£8 Donator rank

Donator perks

In-game permissions and features

  • Access to Water Buckets and Lava Buckets.
  • Fly around using Magic Carpet.
  • Access the Compazz plugin.
  • Create your own Spleef, TNT Run and BlockBall arenas and games.
  • Create custom Warp Signs.
  • Use colours in chat and on signs.
  • Full access to the CraftBook plugin.
  • Access to the /whitelist command.
  • Inspect block history using the LogBlock wooden pickaxe.
  • Set unlimited homes with PersonalWarps.
  • Bypass the /spawn and /warp cooldowns.
  • Bypass item, item-drop and item-break blacklists.
  • Bypass the /wild cooldown (coming soon).

Commands

  • /clearinventory – Clear your inventory.
  • /getpos – View your coordinates.
  • /time – Change the server time.
  • /thru, /ascend and /descend – Move through and between blocks.
  • /ignorebroadcasts – Hide server broadcasts.
  • /cjm – Create custom join and leave messages.

Rank and customisation

  • Receive the [Donator] prefix instead of your normal rank prefix.
  • Request a custom nickname and prefix, including colours.
  • Access the exclusive Supporters Lounge on Discord.

Additional rewards

  • Access to /kit donator.
  • 7,500 Discord server XP.
  • 7,500 in-game server coins.
  • 48 additional Town blocks.
  • One free Nation.

After donating

Open a Discord ticket and ask an Administrator to apply your rewards. Include your Minecraft username and which payment method you used.
